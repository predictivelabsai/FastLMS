# England-first science and chemistry pathway

FastLearn’s science pathway is designed as three connected courses. It starts
with the language and observable phenomena of primary science, moves through
the statutory ideas normally met in England at KS3 and GCSE, and then bridges
to A-level and broad UK first-year chemistry.

## Curriculum map

| Course | Learner stage | Coverage | Guided activities |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary Science | Years 1–6 | Scientific enquiry; materials; states and separation; living things; light, sound and electricity; Earth, space and forces | Particle-state explorer; material suitability |
| Chemistry Fundamentals | KS3–GCSE | Particle model; atoms, ions and isotopes; periodicity; bonding; balanced equations; moles; acids, redox and electrolysis; energetics, rates, resources and analysis | Atom builder; equation balancer; 3D bonding; mole calculation |
| Advanced Chemistry | A-level to year one | Orbitals and spectra; thermodynamics, kinetics and equilibrium; acids and electrochemistry; organic structure, mechanisms and spectroscopy; inorganic periodicity and coordination chemistry; practical uncertainty | 3D VSEPR model; spectra interpretation; mechanism reasoning |

## Reference points

- England National Curriculum science, key stages 1–4
- Department for Education GCSE chemistry subject content
- GCE AS and A-level chemistry subject content
- QAA Chemistry Subject Benchmark Statement (2022) for broad UK university outcomes

University chemistry has no single national first-year syllabus. Advanced
course content therefore uses the shared physical, organic, inorganic and
practical foundations expected across UK programmes rather than claiming an
exam-board-specific university pathway.

## Delivery rules

- English and Estonian are reviewed first. Other languages safely fall back to
  English until their translations are complete.
- Activities are guided and curriculum-authored. Learners do not submit
  arbitrary molecular structures or unvalidated chemical procedures.
- Expected answers remain in `answer_payload` on the server. The browser only
  receives the scenario and display data.
- 3D molecular interaction is supplied by locally vendored 3Dmol.js. A
  keyboard-operable choice/input interface and text description remain usable
  if WebGL is unavailable.
- Simulations teach reasoning and measurement; they do not replace laboratory
  supervision, risk assessment, or school safety procedures.
